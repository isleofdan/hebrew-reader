# Tricky look-ups to try

Words the Look up page used to refuse or get wrong, and what each should
answer now (hebrew-save-one). Type each into the box on
https://hebrew-reader-dan.fly.dev/lookup. The checks run the same list
against a stand-in for the model (`scripts/tricky-lookups.json`); the live
model's words will differ, but the kind of answer should not.

- **אלפית**: a card ("a thousandth"), marked "Likely", with "Other readings"
  under the meaning; tapping one looks that word up.
- **סבבה**: a card ("fine, great, cool") labelled "slang".
- **תירלול**: "No card for this word", then "Did you mean" with words it may
  have meant (each a button that looks the word up), then "Save it".
- **טלפום**: the card of טלפון, with "You typed טלפום".
- **צה"ל**: a card that spells the abbreviation out (the Israel Defense Forces).
- **ביטחון**: the card of ביטחון itself; the ב is not cut off as a prefix.
- **חרצובות**: a rare word; a card marked "A best guess", labelled "literary".
- **ובבית**: the card of בית, read with ו and ב in front.
- **שהממשלה**: the card of ממשלה, read with ש and ה in front.
- **כשהגענו**: the card of הגיע (Hif'il, "when we arrived").
- **ממשלת**: the card of ממשלה, its construct form.
- **בית ספר**: a phrase: "What it means" (a school), its word-for-word sense,
  "Look up each word" with בית and ספר, and "Save it".
- **ח"כ** (typed with a plain "): a card that spells it out (a member of
  Knesset).
- **נתניהו**: a card for a name, saying who it likely is.
- **אינטל**: a card for a company's name.
- **לעשות check**: a phrase: its meaning, then לעשות as a button and "check"
  shown as it is.
- **שָׁלום** (nikud typed on one letter only): the card of שלום.
- **שלומ** (a regular mem at the end): the card of שלום, with "You typed שלומ".
- **ג'ובניק**: a card labelled "army slang".
- **sababa**: "Hebrew words for sababa", with סַבָּבָה first.

Also worth a try: **דו או פרד** (a phrase with its meaning), **take it for
granted** (an English phrase of four words), and "Save it" on a phrase,
then "Put on a desk" from "Looked up".
